# Factoruide Profile

**Your character, for the Factoruide app.** For **WoW Forever**, **Classic Era / Hardcore** and **TBC Anniversary**.

Factoruide computes a leveling guide for your character: your class, professions, zones, dungeons and play style. This addon tells the app where your character actually stands, and take that into account when computing the guide.

> The guides are made in the Factoruide app (desktop, or https://factoruide.brouznouf.com): https://github.com/brouznouf/factoruide
> To follow them in game, install **Factoruide Guide** too, or use Polestar on WoW Forever.

## What it records

- **Profile**: level and XP, quests done and in the log, position, hearthstone, flight paths seen, professions, gear and spells. Kept up to date every minute and at logout.
- **What you see while playing**: quest givers, positions, XP received. It helps make the next guides more accurate. It stays on your computer; the desktop app sends it only if you turn that on.

## Using it

- `/fg` opens the addon's page in the game's options (**AddOns → Factoruide**).
- **Desktop app**: it reads your characters by itself. 
- **Web app**: **Export my character** gives a text. Copy it and paste it in **New guide**.
- **Advanced** page: what was recorded, debug messages, clear the recordings.
